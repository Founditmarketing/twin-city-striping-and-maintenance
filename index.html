What makes us unique

Twin City Striping & Maintenance is your one-stop shop for pavement and pavement maintenance services. We do not turn down any size job. Whether you need a small pothole repair or a large paving project, a single handicap parking spot or several thousand parking spaces, we have you covered.

We like to meet our customers whenever possible and offer custom service plans based on each customer’s situation. This allows us to provide more affordable solutions or long-term, extensive solutions depending on your needs.

When you call, you will speak directly with us. In some cases, we can meet the same day and provide service the same week. We are locally and family owned, and we take great pride in our work and in your experience with us.

We primarily serve North Louisiana, South Arkansas, and Midwestern Mississippi, but we are willing to travel anywhere.

Call today for an evaluation on any asphalt paving, asphalt repairs, driveways, concrete repairs, pavement markings, parking bumpers, traffic signage, power washing, rock work, crack and joint sealing, seal coating, and more.
Call or text 318-614-3139.
